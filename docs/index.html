About the app

Gaming News is a free Android application that gathers video game news from publicly available RSS feeds published by gaming news outlets, and lists upcoming game releases using the IGDB database. It is a news aggregator: it does not produce original editorial content. Every article is attributed to its original publisher and opens on the publisher's own website.

What you can do with it

  • Browse the latest gaming news by platform (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, PC) or all together.
  • Search articles and save the ones you want to read later.
  • See a calendar of upcoming game releases.
  • Enable an optional daily reminder notification, dark theme, and data-saving image loading.

Where the content comes from

Headlines, summaries, and images are retrieved from the public RSS feeds of their respective publishers, including Eurogamer, VG247, Kotaku, Video Games Chronicle, Polygon, PC Gamer, Rock Paper Shotgun, PCGamesN, Push Square, PlayStation Blog, Xbox Wire, Pure Xbox, XboxEra, Nintendo Life, My Nintendo News, Nintendo Wire, and their Spanish-language counterparts (Eurogamer España, Vandal, 3DJuegos, VidaExtra, Areajugones, PureGaming, Nintenderos, and others). All trademarks and content belong to their respective owners. Release information is provided by IGDB. If you are a rights holder and want your feed removed, contact us.
