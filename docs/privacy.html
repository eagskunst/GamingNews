Summary

Gaming News does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, address, or phone number. Your reading preferences and saved articles stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • App settings (dark theme, image loading, daily reminder).
  • Articles you save, and a short-lived cache of downloaded feeds and release data.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes this data.

Data processed by third parties

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Cloud Messaging): aggregated usage and crash-free operation metrics, plus a device push token used to deliver notifications. See the Firebase privacy documentation.
  • News publishers: when the app downloads a feed or you open an article, the publisher's servers receive your IP address and standard request data, as with any web request.
  • IGDB / Twitch: requests for upcoming game release information.
  • Google Play: distribution and, when you consent, crash reporting.

No personal data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Permissions

The app requests internet and network state access to download feeds, and notification permission only if you enable the optional daily reminder.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights and requests

To ask what data is associated with your installation, or to request its deletion, email contact@eagskunst.com. We reply within 3 business days.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
